Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: 2025-08-05  |  最后更新：2025年8月5日

This Privacy Policy applies to PaleoLine (BioGraph — 古生物漫游图谱), the mobile application published by Yang Rui.

English

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following data to provide core app functionality:

  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Search queries — species names you search are transmitted to the Wikipedia API (wikipedia.org) to retrieve encyclopedia summaries and images. These queries are sent directly from your device; we do not log or store them.
  • Account data — if you choose to create an account and log in, we collect the username you provide and the knowledge graphs you create and publish. You can delete your account and associated data at any time via the app settings.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location (GPS).
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, photos, or microphone.
  • We do not collect device fingerprints, installed app lists, or browsing history.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device information and Advertising ID. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Wikipedia API — species search requests are sent to wikipedia.org to fetch summaries and images. See Wikimedia's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

App preferences (language, saved graphs) are stored locally on your device. Account data is stored on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: yangruiyangdan@gmail.com

中文

1. 我们收集的数据

我们收集以下数据以提供核心功能：

  • 广告标识符（Advertising ID） — 用于 Google AdMob 展示个性化或非个性化广告。您可以在设备的 设置 → Google → 广告 中随时重置广告标识符或关闭广告个性化。
  • 搜索查询 — 您搜索的物种名称会直接发送至 Wikipedia API（wikipedia.org）以获取百科摘要和图片。这些查询从您的设备直接发出，我们不会记录或存储。
  • 账户数据 — 如果您选择注册账号并登录，我们会收集您提供的用户名以及您创建并发布的知识图谱。您可以随时在应用设置中删除账户及关联数据。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 我们不收集您的精确位置（GPS）。
  • 我们不访问您的通讯录、短信、相册或麦克风。
  • 我们不收集设备指纹、已安装应用列表或浏览记录。

3. 第三方服务

  • Google AdMob — 用于展示广告。AdMob 可能收集设备信息和广告标识符。参阅 Google 隐私权政策。
  • Wikipedia API — 物种搜索请求发送至 wikipedia.org 以获取摘要和图片。参阅 维基媒体隐私政策。

4. 数据存储与安全

应用偏好设置（语言、保存的图谱）存储在您的设备本地。账户数据存储在我们的服务器上。数据传输使用业界标准的加密协议（HTTPS/TLS）。

5. 您的权利

您可随时通过下方邮箱请求访问、更正或删除您的个人数据。我们将在 30 天内回复。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题：yangruiyangdan@gmail.com
